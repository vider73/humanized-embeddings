      • 104 axes in 9 families (physics → matter → life → senses → emotion → cognition → society → metaphysics → action), each anchored by two absolute extremes (0.0 / 1.0)

      • Train a small MLP to predict the 104 scores straight from the 384-dim MiniLM embedding
      • Honesty loop: 30_normalize.py flags dead/correlated axes, steering/ proves which are causally real → both feed back into axis design
